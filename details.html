  • Vulnerability to mammalian predators

    Nesting females are especially vulnerable to mammalian predators (stoats, possums, rats) due to their nest being at, or close to, ground level. In addition, during their moulting season, they lose a significant amount of feathers limiting their ability to fly, leaving them particularly vulnerable.
